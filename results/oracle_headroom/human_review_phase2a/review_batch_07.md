## Question

```text
When analyzing volatility dynamics between the European Union Allowance and natural gas markets in the second phase of the EU Emissions Trading Scheme, which direction of the estimated cross-market coefficients demonstrates significant influence?
```

## Gold / Reference

```text
In EUA-gas during Phase II of the EU ETS, the estimated cross-market coefficient β12 (0.0140) is statistically significant at the 5% level, while β21 is not significant. This demonstrates significant volatility spillover from the natural gas market to the EU Allowances (EUA) market, but not from EUA to natural gas. Therefore, the significant influence is from natural gas to EUA.
```
